Hi! I'm back from my break and last week we celebrated our two years of having a BIPOC Craft Night! We meet every month usually on the third Monday and you can find more info [here](https://arisamakes.com/bipoccraftcircle/).

Time sure flies by. It didn't feel that long ago that we didn't know where to host and we weren't sure if people were going to come. I was so nervous about starting the group! Now I've made so many friends through this group I get to see every month and learn about different crafts from them.

I do love that we're starting a tradition of celebrating the start of craft night every November. It really does help me feel grateful that I have this community!

![Ube brownie with two lit candles](../../assets/blog/img/HappyTwoYears.jpeg 'Ube brownie with two lit candles')

We had ube brownies and vegan matcha cookies that were so tasty.

![Sewn bookmarks in a black and white Nightmare before Christmas fabric and a list of BIPOC authors and books](../../assets/blog/img/bookmarks.jpeg 'Sewn bookmarks in a black and white Nightmare before Christmas fabric and a list of BIPOC authors and books')

A friend sewed some bookmarks for everyone with a list of BIPOC authors and books.

![Illustration of a quilt in blue and orange and also says "Happy Two Years of BIPOC Craft Nights"](../../assets/blog/img/QuiltingCardIllustration.jpeg 'Illustration of a quilt in blue and orange')

And then, at the very last minute, I decided to sketch some cards with personalized illustrations of each person's craft. Somehow I made 13 different cards in one day?? I had a lot of fun making them even if my back, shoulder, and hands hurt afterwards.
