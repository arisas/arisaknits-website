## Updates to the leg section

So.. I realized while trying to explain how to do the Leg section that I had completely messed up the instructions for the Right Leg and Foot. I probably spent hours this past week following the pattern I wrote, frogging and crocheting it again to test it. My biggest worry was that I was twisting my stitches for the legs since it's important to keep them straight and all lined up for the foot to be worked correctly.

Since lining up the stitches is especially important for the leg section: I highly recommend watching [this video "Easy Trick for Lining Up Amigurumi Stitches Every Time!"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?si=GBVORs6WpEYySWtJ&t=205&v=V2q8p1k6cWY&feature=youtu.be) on how to do the yarn under and yarn over method and making sure you're pulling the yarn up instead of to the side when crocheting.

### Left Leg Section

Here are some photos I added to the pattern that I can explain more about it here.

![Row 17 of the Left Leg section](../../assets/blog/img/Row17.png 'Row 17 of the Left Leg section')

This photo is where I just finished row 17 and about to start row 18 for the Left Leg section. It's basically showing where you'll want to join the round at that 5th stitch.

![Row 18 of the Left Leg section](../../assets/blog/img/Row18.png 'Row 18 of the Left Leg section')

This next photo is me working row 18 of the Left Leg section where I'm about to slst to join the round for the left leg.

The stitch marker in both photos is where the original start of the row is. The new start of the row will be that slst.

To explain it differently: With 18 sts after finishing row 17, you want to sc stitches #1-4, skip stitches #5-14, slst stitch #15, and sc stitches #16-18. Once you've done these steps, you should now only have 8 stitches you're working with for the left leg.

### Right Leg Section

![Row 18 of the Right Leg section](../../assets/blog/img/RightRow18.png 'Row 18 of the Right Leg section')

This photo is me working row 18 of the Right Leg section and where you need to slst.

The Right Foot should now be worked where the front of the foot on row 30 is increasing stitches while the back is only sc.
