AHHH I'm done tying up and warping my countermarch loom! I bought my Glimakra Ideal back in April (holy, it's been 5 months) and I spent the last few weeks slowly tying it up for the very first time. If you know anything about weaving, supposedly countermarch looms are a lot of work when it comes to tying up compared to other types of looms. I assume this is because there are so many parts- short lamms, long lamms, treadles, connecting them to the heddles, and connecting to the countermarch system at the top.

## Beaming The Warp

I thought I was almost done setting up my loom a few weeks ago BUT I sent the photo below to an experienced weaver friend and turns out my lease stick were too thick and the warp sticks need to be arranged evenly so that the warp has more of a consistent tension. (Thank you friend-- I don't know what I'd do without your guidance!!)

![My countermarch back of loom](../../assets/blog/img/BackofLoom.jpeg 'My countermarch back of loom')

This is the mess that was at the back of my loom 😳 And below is what the back of my loom looks like now. All even tension!

![Finished rebeam my warp](../../assets/blog/img/RebeamingWarpDone.jpeg 'Finished rebeam my warp')

It took me a while to make sure there weren't any tangles and that the warp was threaded in the correct order. Anddd turns out it was not threaded in the correct order BTW and I'm so glad I double-checked before rebeaming the warp.

![Starting to rebeam my warp](../../assets/blog/img/RebeamingWarp.jpeg 'Starting to rebeam my warp')

My friend sent me some links for [tying the lease sticks](https://peggyosterkamp.com/2020/10/tying-the-lease-sticks-separately/) from Peggy Osterkamp's Weaving Blog and [how to warp from back to front](https://warpedforgood.com/2016/03/quiet-friday-warping-back-to-front-with-confidence/) from Warped for Good.

Following the Warped for Good instructions:

1. I placed one warp stick on each of the 8 flat sides on my octagonal warp beam. There should be 8 sticks, one on each flat side.
2. Make two full turns where I passed all 8 flat sides without placing any warp sticks TWICE
3. Follow the steps again until you're done beaming the warp!

## Tying up my Glimakra Ideal Countermarch loom

Many of the ladies at the Weavers Guild recommended this book I ended up borrowing from the Guild: [Tying Up The Countermarch Loom by Joanne Hall](https://www.eugenetextilecenter.com/tying-up-the-countermarch-loom). I highly recommend it for anyone tying up their loom!

Since I'm a visual learner, I also watched a few videos to better understand what I was reading and the illustrations. This video [How to Assemble the Glimåkra Julia Loom](https://youtu.be/uVecplfCubU?si=F8SOzaANeoYTBwbk) was the most helpful for me as it has both instructions on the warp and tying up everything. Unfortunately, he doesn't show how to tie up the lamms and treadles, so I'll share below on how I figured that out.

## Tying up the lamms to the treadles

[Tying Up The Countermarch Loom by Joanne Hall](https://www.eugenetextilecenter.com/tying-up-the-countermarch-loom) has helpful guidance on how to read a tie-up draft. The [Weaving Hand Towels Simply](https://woolery.com/products/weaving-hand-towels-simply) book that I borrowed from the library also has great info on how to read weaving drafts-- I learned that you'll need to check if the draft is written for jack looms or countermarch, but they're easy to convert. [Converting tie-ups](https://weavingwithjanetdawson.com/blog/2020/10/18/converting-tie-ups/?fbclid=PAVERFWATy3h9wZG9mAmZkaWQWUMwM56Jc6Nl1GpLSBcUKW9sebXTUzWV4dG4DYWVtAjEwAHNydGMGYXBwX2lkDzEyNDAyNDU3NDI4NzQxNAABp2CkwjIFpPwJRhsLSb86mSt9HrDFklOh8AVlq0k3NZ3J430kJyexrrhgVrvC_aem_ARckUoZ0owdl6rHyzgkiag) will tell you how to convert between drafts.

I drew a little illustration on how I was going to tie up the lamms and the treadle to process all of this info. This whole thing feels like a puzzle game and now I'm inspired to try making a game that's just reading a draft and setting up your lamms and treadles haha.

![My illustration on tying up the lamms and treadles](../../assets/blog/img/IllustrationLammsTreadles.jpeg 'My illustration on tying up the lamms and treadles')

## Warp board

Since I needed to have my warp ready as part of the tie up, I had to look up some instructions on how to use a warp board.

The video [How to Assemble the Glimåkra Julia Loom](https://youtu.be/uVecplfCubU?si=F8SOzaANeoYTBwbk) had some instructions on this but the [10 Tips for Using a Warping Board](https://youtu.be/IW9B7khlWTY?si=zzQ9_Ai3yQNwZN6z) had helpful illustrations on where the threads cross.

Anyway, happy weaving! Hope this helps anyone that's warping and tying up their countermarch loom for the first time. I feel like there are resources all over the place and it takes a while to find them all!
