I'm starting a monthly showcase of all the random art things I worked on each month. Here are my September creations!

## Countermarch loom weaving

![Practicing even weaving on the Glimakra Ideal loom](../../assets/blog/img/EvenWeaving.jpeg 'Practicing even weaving on the Glimakra Ideal loom')

Now that I finished tying up and warping my Glimakra Ideal loom, I've been practicing on it just to get the hang of the beater, treadles, and making sure my weaving is even. It's looking a lot better now that my friend suggested I pull the beater from the center.

I'm using some white Cotton Slub Spiral yarn I got for free from the Seattle Weavers Guild.

## Knitting the Magical Girl Transformation Scarf from Katharina Kuntz

![Magical Girl Scarf in progress](../../assets/blog/img/MagicalGirlScarf.jpeg 'Magical Girl Scarf in progress')

I've been slowly making my friend a Sailor Moon scarf-- the pattern is by [Katharina Kuntz](https://katharina-kuntz.myshopify.com/products/magical-girl-scarf-signs-knitting-pattern) who's known for a bunch of beautiful double knit patterned scarves and you can find the pattern here. It's my first time doing double knitting and I highly recommend getting the Norwegian Knitting ring to help make sure your tension is good. It's looking so beautiful already and I want to design my own double knit pattern now!

### I'm using:

- Size 0 knitting needles
- The Wandering Flock fingering weight wool yarn in colors [**100s & 1000s**](https://thewanderingflock.com/products/fingering-singles?variant=44488247443673) and [**Navy**](https://thewanderingflock.com/products/fingering-singles?variant=44086389342425)

## Owl sketching and watercolor class

![Owl watercolor painting](../../assets/blog/img/OwlPainting.jpeg 'Owl watercolor painting')

I took a class on how to sketch and watercolor owls with [ipaintbirbs](https://lizclaytonfuller.com/) online! It was an amazing class and I felt like I learned so much about watercolor in just 1.5 hours. Starting a drawing from the owl's beak is the way to go.

## Sketches of my dogs

![Sketches of my dogs Cheyenne and Dundee](../../assets/blog/img/DogSketches.jpeg 'Sketches of my dogs Cheyenne and Dundee')

I did some sketches of my dogs at a weekly art jam. This is probably one of the best sketches I've done with my dogs and it's thanks to the owl painting class with ipaintbirbs! I did something similar to the owl class where I started with my dogs nose and measured the distance between their eyes, etc. from there to keep the proportions right.

## Painting Halloween-themed houses

![Halloween Themed Houses](../../assets/blog/img/HalloweenHouse.jpeg 'Halloween Themed Houses')

My friend hosted us to paint some bird houses and turn them into Halloween themed houses to use as decorations! All of our houses were so cute and I forgot how relaxing it was to paint something.

## Crocheting the Formation Vest from KnotBadBritt

![Formation Vest](../../assets/blog/img/FormationVest.jpeg 'Formation Vest')

I'm making my very first vest and using yarn that's not wool (yay!). [This pattern by KnotBadBritt](https://www.knotbadbritt.com/patterns/p/formation-vest-crochet-pattern) was seriously so addicting and fun. It's worked sideways in one continuous panel which I've never done before for a shirt, but this might be my favorite way of doing it?!

I still need to block it so that I can sew the seams and ribbing for the neck and sleeves.

### I'm using:

- Size 4mm crochet hooks
- The Wandering Flock sport weight cotton/mulberry silk/linen yarn in the color [**Poppy**](https://thewanderingflock.com/products/cotton-lino?variant=46626218115289)
